# Product Overview

## AI Consultant Platform

A web-based AI consultation platform that connects users with virtual industry-specific consultants for business matching and talent recommendation services.

## Core Features

- **Virtual AI Consultants**: Industry-specialized AI consultants with deep domain expertise across various sectors (IT, Manufacturing, Finance, Healthcare, HR)
- **Real-time Voice Consultation**: Interactive voice-based conversation system with speech synthesis and recognition capabilities
- **Intelligent Conversation Flow**: Multi-phase consultation process including questioning, hot reading, cold reading, subsidy recommendations, and summary
- **Talent Matching System**: AI-powered talent recommendation based on consultation context and business needs
- **Consultation History**: Complete conversation history tracking with recommended talents for future reference

## Target Use Case

### Primary Scenarios
- **Business Talent Acquisition**: Companies seeking specialized talent recommendations based on their industry needs
- **Business Matching**: Organizations looking for strategic business partnerships and connections
- **Subsidy Consultation**: Businesses seeking guidance on available subsidies and grants
- **Industry-Specific Advisory**: Companies requiring specialized industry knowledge and network connections

### User Types
- **Small to Medium Enterprises**: Seeking cost-effective consultation for business development
- **Startups**: Looking for talent and business connections to scale operations
- **HR Departments**: Requiring specialized talent sourcing and recommendations
- **Business Development Teams**: Needing industry connections and partnership opportunities

## Key Value Proposition

### Unique Benefits
- **24/7 Availability**: Access to AI consultants anytime without scheduling constraints
- **Industry Expertise**: Consultants with deep specialization in specific industries (IT, Manufacturing, Finance, Healthcare, HR)
- **Network Access**: Virtual access to extensive professional networks (CTOs, executives, industry leaders)
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Context-aware talent and business matching based on consultation dialogue
- **Cost-Effective**: Eliminates traditional consulting fees while providing expert-level guidance
- **Conversation Intelligence**: Advanced conversation flow with hot/cold reading techniques for better understanding

### Competitive Differentiators
- **Voice-First Interface**: Natural voice conversation rather than text-only interaction
- **Multi-Phase Consultation**: Structured consultation process that mirrors human consultant methodology
- **Real-Time Response**: Immediate answers and recommendations without waiting periods
- **Historical Learning**: Access to past consultations for continuous improvement and reference
- **Specialized Networks**: Each consultant brings unique industry connections and expertise
